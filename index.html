About the Asia Pacific Chapter

The ISPD Asia Pacific Chapter serves as the regional hub for peritoneal dialysis professionals across Asia and the Pacific. Our chapter works closely with the ISPD CEO and Executive Committee to align regional initiatives with global standards.

We are committed to promoting excellence in PD care through education, research collaboration, and advocacy for patient access to quality treatment.
