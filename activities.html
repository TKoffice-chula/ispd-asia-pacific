Activities & Events

Programs, discussions, and upcoming congresses

Home / Activities

PD Case Discussion

Our regular PD Case Discussion sessions bring together clinicians from across the Asia Pacific region to review challenging cases, share clinical insights, and learn from peer experiences. These interactive forums are held online to maximize accessibility.

  • Monthly virtual case conferences covering complex PD complications
  • Multidisciplinary participation including nephrologists, nurses, and surgeons
  • Case submissions welcomed from member institutions
  • Expert commentary from regional and international PD leaders

Upcoming ISPD Events

Stay informed about upcoming ISPD congresses, endorsed meetings, and regional workshops happening across the Asia Pacific.

  • ISPD 2026 Global Congress — Cape Town, South Africa (October 2026). The flagship international congress of the International Society for Peritoneal Dialysis. Asia-Pacific members are encouraged to attend and submit abstracts. Details and registration: ispd2026.org.za.
  • Regional Workshops: Hands-on training sessions for PD catheter insertion, peritonitis management, and automated PD programming.
  • Webinar Series: Regular online lectures on emerging topics in PD research and clinical practice.

Life-time Achievement Award

The ISPD Asia Pacific Chapter Life-time Achievement Award recognizes individuals who have made exceptional and sustained contributions to the advancement of peritoneal dialysis in the Asia Pacific region.

  • Nominations are accepted annually from chapter members.
  • Recipients are honored at the ISPD Congress or a regional meeting.
  • The award celebrates excellence in clinical practice, research, education, or advocacy.

Associated Society Activities

We collaborate with national and regional nephrology societies to promote PD at the local level and ensure our activities complement existing programs.

  • Joint symposia at national nephrology congresses
  • Co-branded educational materials and guidelines translation
  • Shared fellowship and travel grant opportunities
  • Collaborative multi-center research initiatives
