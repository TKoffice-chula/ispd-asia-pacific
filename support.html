Home / Support

Fellowship & Fellowship Scholar Programs

The ISPD Asia Pacific Chapter offers fellowship opportunities designed to support early-career nephrologists and researchers in gaining specialized training in peritoneal dialysis.

  • Clinical Fellowship: Observe and train at high-volume PD centers in the region for 1–3 months.
  • Research Fellowship: Collaborate on ongoing multi-center studies or develop independent research proposals.
  • Fellowship Scholar Awards: Competitive scholarships covering travel, accommodation, and stipend for selected candidates.

Applications are reviewed twice yearly. Priority is given to candidates from low- and middle-income countries and those with clear career development plans in PD.

ISPD Travel Grant Support

Travel grants are available to support ISPD Asia Pacific Chapter members in attending the ISPD Congress, regional workshops, and endorsed educational meetings.

  • Grants typically cover registration fees, airfare, and accommodation.
  • Applicants must be current chapter members in good standing.
  • Preference for first-time congress attendees and abstract presenters.
  • Application deadlines align with major congress schedules.

To apply, download the application form below and submit it together with your CV and a letter of support from your department head.
