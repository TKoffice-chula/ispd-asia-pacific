Submission System

We welcome contributions from members and the broader PD community. Accepted content includes:

  • Clinical case reports with teaching points
  • Original research summaries and abstracts
  • Review articles on PD-related topics
  • News from national societies and PD programs
  • Member profiles and career milestones

Submissions should be sent via email to newsletter@ispd-asia.org with "Newsletter Submission" in the subject line. Please include a brief author biography and any relevant disclosures.
